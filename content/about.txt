About Gopher Graveyard
======================

You are reading this over the Gopher protocol — or through a web
gateway that speaks it for you, because your browser no longer can.

This gopherhole is served by gopherd, a Gopher server written from
scratch in Go for Kiroween 2026 (category: Resurrection).

The protocol, in full (RFC 1436):
  1. Connect to TCP port 70.
  2. Send one line: the selector. Empty means "the root menu".
  3. The server sends text or a menu, then a lone "." and hangs up.
No headers. No cookies. No JavaScript. No state.
