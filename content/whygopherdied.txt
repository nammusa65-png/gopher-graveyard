Why Gopher died
===============

1991  Gopher is born at the University of Minnesota. Menus of text,
      organised like a filesystem, searchable with Veronica.
1993  The Web arrives with pictures and free-form hypertext.
      Minnesota announces licensing fees for Gopher servers;
      the Web's CERN puts its technology in the public domain.
1995+ Browsers fold Gopher in as a curiosity (gopher:// links),
      then quietly remove support. Firefox drops it in 2011,
      Chrome never restores it.

What survived: a few hundred servers, kept alive by enthusiasts.
A January 2026 census counted 411 active servers and about
5.86 million selectors. Small — but not zero. Not dead. Undead.
